`mu rip` uses `@hansogj/discogs-item-lookup` 1.5.0: accepts master IDs (`m1439`), and names the album folder from the master's original year and title (a 1989 reissue of *In Rock* rips to `Deep Purple/1970 Deep Purple In Rock`). The cover is fetched from the resolved release, so a master ID no longer reaches discogs-cover.
